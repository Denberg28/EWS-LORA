## Bench procedure

Use two supported boards with antennas, USB power, a normally open momentary switch, a computer with two serial monitors and optionally a 3.3 V logic USB–UART adapter. Follow README to flash and pair. Keep testing within authorized RF conditions. Keep USB logs locally, with timestamps, at 115200. Capture button and LED on video for latency measurements. Use a fresh test key and conduct the exercise with a staffed receiver and no expectation of dispatch.

1. **Startup and input.** Flash and provision each role. Repeat normal reset 10 times including five held-button resets. Expect no unsolicited SOS. Twenty taps shorter than 1 second must send zero alerts. A full activation hold should latch SOS within 2.5 seconds of pressing.
2. **Clear path and timings.** With both boards ready, antennas attached and at least 20 seconds since any TX, do ten SOS/CLEAR cycles. Measure from SOS latch to receiver saved indication and beacon ACK. All ten should show saved receipt and matching ACK within 2 seconds of latch in a clean RF environment. Hold 5 seconds to clear each cycle. Expect REMOTE CLEAR and READY within 25 seconds after the clear hold completes. Repeat once with a fresh SOS during pending clear while the receiver is off, then restore receiver and confirm the newer SOS wins.
3. **GNSS acquisition/loss.** With no module, get an acknowledged SOS marked NO GPS FIX. For a repeatable input, wire 3.3 V UART TX/GND to GPIO44/GND and run `python scripts/nmea_fixture.py PORT`. Verify receiver shows latitude +12.50000 and longitude +123.50000 within 35 seconds. Stop the fixture. Verify NO GPS FIX within 45 seconds. Repeat using real GNSS outdoors and compare to its reported position within one rounding unit (0.00001°). Check that using no module never blocks alerting.
4. **Link outage.** Power receiver off. Activate SOS. Verify WAIT ACK and at least two retries 20 ± 2 seconds apart. Restore receiver. Expect matching ACK within 25 seconds. While ACKed, remove receiver power again. Expect WAIT ACK within 35 seconds of the last ACK and a saved SOS on receiver reboot. Restore and confirm ACK recovery. Test repeated power cuts in both directions.
5. **Persistence.** During active SOS and then pending clear, power-cycle the beacon three times each before ACK. Verify it resumes the same intent and uses a higher sequence after boot. Power-cycle the receiver after receiving SOS and again after CLEAR. Verify restored OLED state with the receiver disconnected from beacon RF. Cycle receiver power near the first ACK and confirm a repeated report is eventually acknowledged without a second newly saved event.
6. **Isolation and faults.** Provision a second test pair with a different key. Their alerts must not produce a matching ACK or change the original receiver state. With an additional programmable SX1262 and a private bench harness, inject one bit-flipped authenticated frame, a duplicate, an old valid SOS after CLEAR, and an old ACK after beacon retransmission. Only a duplicate of the currently saved report may cause an ACK. Break the OLED bus on a spare fixture or disconnect an external test OLED, then verify LED, logging and radio operation. Exercise an injected SPI fault and recovery on a spare bench fixture with the equipment powered down during rewiring. Verify fault indication and no false ACK. Use an instrumented development build with a deliberate loop stall to measure watchdog reset within 10 seconds, and repeat basic persistence checks on the release image.
7. **Endurance and coverage.** Run a continuous 8-hour bench SOS with matching ACKs and synthetic moving fixes if available. Expect zero unexpected resets, no lost incident state and at least 99% received scheduled attempts under a clean link. Repeat over the actual deployment routes and log distance, obstacles, terrain, RSSI if collected separately, time and missed responses. Set a verified service boundary and minimum battery runtime before deployment.
8. **Delivery.** Flash both published merged images, verify the displayed role, pair and perform SOS/ACK/CLEAR. Reflash the same role without erasing and verify pair retention and active-state recovery in an isolated exercise. Restore a saved previous image or wipe the dedicated test units and reinstall both roles with a new key. Verify end-to-end receipt after rollback/reinstall.
